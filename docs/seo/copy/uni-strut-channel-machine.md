## H1
Multi-Size Uni-Strut Channel Roll Forming Machine

## Intro (lead paragraph)
Produce solar-grade strut channels in the three most-demanded sizes on a single line with the Super
Rollforming Multi-Size Uni-Strut Channel Roll Forming Machine. Engineered for **41×21, 41×41, and
41×61** channel production, it forms galvanized steel, stainless steel, and aluminium into precise,
load-ready strut profiles with PLC-controlled hydraulic cutting — ideal for solar mounting structures
and general steel framing. A 14-station HCHCR roll set holds profile quality through long,
high-volume runs, backed by 50+ years of roll-forming manufacturing.

## Key benefits
- **Three channel sizes, one machine** — switch between 41×21, 41×41, and 41×61 without a separate line.
- **High throughput** — up to ~10 tons/day for solar EPC and structural-supply volumes.
- **PLC-controlled hydraulic cut** — electrically operated, adjustable cut-to-length for accurate, repeatable channels.
- **Solar-ready metals** — forms 1–2 mm galvanized steel, stainless steel, and aluminium.
- **Durable 14-station tooling** — HCHCR rolls for long-lasting, consistent sheet formation quality.
- **Lower labour, less waste** — automated forming and cutting cut manual handling and scrap.

## Applications
