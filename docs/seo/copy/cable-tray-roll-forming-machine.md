## Intro (lead paragraph)
Manufacture perforated and plain cable trays across a full size range on one line with the Super
Rollforming Cable Tray Roll Forming Machine. Its interchangeable-rolls technology forms trays from
**100 mm to 1000 mm** without a separate machine for every width, combining automatic punching,
precise roll forming, and hydraulic cut-to-length in a continuous, high-uptime process. Built for
long production hours and consistent tray geometry, it's engineered by a roll-forming manufacturer
with 50+ years of experience.

## Key benefits
- **One line, many sizes** — interchangeable rolls cover 100–1000 mm cable-tray profiles, so you
  quote more jobs without buying more machines.
- **High output** — up to ~5 tons/day of finished tray for volume production.
- **Automatic punching built in** — perforation slots formed in-line, no separate punching step.
- **Wide gauge range** — forms 0.5–3 mm material for light-duty to heavy-duty trays.
