## H1
Deck Forming Machine

## Intro (lead paragraph)
Produce precision metal floor- and roof-deck profiles at industrial scale with the Super
Rollforming Deck Forming Machine. Engineered for continuous, high-speed roll forming of
galvanized steel, stainless steel, and aluminium coil into consistent composite-deck and
roof-deck sheets, it delivers clean profiles, accurate rib geometry, and minimal scrap — order
after order. Backed by 50+ years of roll-forming manufacturing experience, it's built to run in
demanding construction-supply and steel-fabrication environments.

## Key benefits (bulleted highlights)
- **High-throughput output** — up to ~3 tons/day of finished deck sheet on a single line.
- **Consistent, true profiles** — precision roll stations and an alignment system hold rib
  geometry sheet to sheet for reliable composite action on site.
- **Handles real construction metals** — forms 0.5–2 mm galvanized steel, stainless steel, and aluminium.
- **Integrated hydraulic shear** — electrically operated, adjustable cut-to-length for clean,
  square ends with no secondary trimming.
- **Heavy-duty build** — 170 mm diameter 14HCHCR forming rolls and a rigid steel frame made to
  hold tolerance under continuous load.
- **Simple, safe operation** — intuitive control panel, emergency stops, and guarding so a single
  operator can run production confidently.

## Applications
- Composite **floor decking** for multi-storey and industrial buildings
- **Roof deck** / structural roofing sheet
- Steel-building and PEB (pre-engineered building) supply
- Infrastructure, warehouse, and commercial construction projects
- Metal-sheet fabricators and roll-forming job-work shops

## FAQ
**What does a deck forming machine make?**
It roll-forms metal coil into floor- and roof-deck sheets — the ribbed profiles used as permanent
formwork for composite concrete floors and as structural roof decking.

**What sheet thickness and materials can it form?**
0.5–2 mm coil in galvanized steel, stainless steel, and aluminium, at widths of 800–1000 mm.

**How fast is it and how much can it produce?**
The line runs at approximately 18 m/min, giving output of around 3 tons of finished deck sheet per day.

**Can the deck profile be customised?**
Yes. The roll tooling is built to your deck profile and rib pattern — share your drawing for a tailored quote.

**Do you deliver and support outside Udaipur / across India / for export?**
Yes — we supply across India and for export. Contact us with your location and profile for delivery and support details.
