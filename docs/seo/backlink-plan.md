# Backlink plan — superrollforming.com

Goal: build relevant, durable referring domains that pass topical authority in the **roll
forming / sheet-metal machinery** niche and reinforce the India + export B2B profile. Quality and
relevance over volume — a handful of trade-relevant links beats hundreds of generic directories.

## Tier 1 — Foundational citations & profiles (do first, this month)
These are low-effort, high-trust, and reinforce the LocalBusiness/NAP consistency (Name, Address,
Phone must match the site and Google Business Profile exactly).

- **Google Business Profile** (also a ranking asset, not just a link).
- **B2B marketplaces (India):** IndiaMART, TradeIndia, ExportersIndia, Justdial, Sulekha — create/verify a rich company profile with all products. These drive real B2B leads *and* links.
- **Export marketplaces:** Alibaba, Made-in-China, Go4WorldBusiness, eWorldTrade — if export is a goal.
- **Business directories:** Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, Hotfrog, IndiaCatalog.
- **Social profiles** (already have FB/IG/YouTube) — ensure each links back to the site and is in the schema `sameAs` (done in the mu-plugin).

> Keep NAP identical across every listing. Inconsistent phone/address dilutes local signals.

## Tier 2 — Industry & trade relevance (weeks 2–8)
Where roll-forming buyers and the trade actually are.

- **Industry associations & directories:** engineering/machinery bodies, MSME/Udyam listings, RIICO/industrial-area business listings (you're in RIICO Gudli, Udaipur).
- **Trade publications & portals** covering metal forming, construction machinery, solar mounting, cable management — pitch a product spotlight or expert quote.
- **Supplier/partner pages:** ask steel suppliers, PLC/motor vendors, and distributors you work with for a "customers/partners" link.
- **YouTube → site:** you already publish machine videos; add the product URL in every video description and pin a comment. Embed those videos on the matching product pages (you do this on the homepage — extend to products).

## Tier 3 — Content-driven / earned links (ongoing, month 2+)
Assets other sites want to reference. Pair with the content in the action plan.

- **Definitive guides:** "Cable Tray Roll Forming Machine — Complete Buyer's Guide", "How Roll Forming Works", "C vs Z Purlin: which machine". Linkable, and they rank.
- **Spec/comparison tables & calculators** (e.g. a coil-width / profile calculator) — tools earn links.
- **Case studies** with named clients (you show "Our Satisfied Clients") — client sites often link back.
- **Digital PR / HARO-style:** answer journalist queries on manufacturing, solar infrastructure, Make-in-India engineering; offer the founder's 50-years perspective.
- **Guest posts** on construction, solar, and manufacturing blogs — one genuinely useful article per target, contextual link to the relevant product/guide.

## Prospecting workflow (use the OpenSEO skill once authenticated)
Run `/link-prospecting` to:
1. Pull backlink profiles of 2–4 competing Indian roll-forming machine manufacturers.
2. Find domains linking to **them but not you** (link gap) — those are proven, relevant prospects.
3. Surface directories, marketplaces, and trade sites in the niche.
4. Discover contact paths and draft outreach.

Also run `/competitive-landscape` to see which content themes earn competitors their links, then
build the better version.

## Outreach basics
- Personalize: reference their site/content, say why the link helps *their* readers.
- Lead with a linkable asset (a guide, a video, a spec sheet), not "please link to us".
- Prioritize relevance + traffic potential over Domain Rating alone.
- Track prospects, status, and won links in a simple sheet; review monthly.

## Avoid
Paid link networks, mass low-quality directory blasts, exact-match-anchor spam, and irrelevant
foreign-language link farms — these risk penalties and waste budget. Relevance is the whole game
in a niche this specific.
